PunkHam
Mayhem S1

Participation Economy · Season System · Browser-Native · Base

The Problem

A digital community with no daily engagement mechanic - members had no structural reason to return after joining, leading to progressive activity decline and increasing sell pressure on community assets.

The Solution

A fully browser-based persistent game world with two participation tiers, a daily action-budget pacing system, and automated 4-week competitive seasons that reset engagement every month.

  • Daily Participation Budget: Each participant receives 10 actions per day, replenishing at one per hour. Guaranteed daily return trigger - members come back to use their budget - while preventing bot farming and burnout that kills unconstrained systems.
  • Territory Control for Passive Income: Asset-holding members claim virtual business locations that generate community currency while offline. Converts holding a digital asset into an ongoing income source - making holding more valuable than selling.
  • Competitive Season with Automatic Reset: 4-week competition cycles with automatic prize payouts and leaderboard resets requiring zero admin action. Creates 12 natural re-engagement windows per year.
Business Impact

Replaces launch-spike activity patterns with a consistent daily engagement rhythm. Automated season resets deliver ongoing urgency without operational overhead.

Punk
Arcade

Automated Game Platform · Zero-Admin Operations · Two Live Games · Base

The Problem

Game platforms that require constant manual operation create operational bottlenecks, erode community trust when operators are unavailable, and cannot scale.

The Solution

Two fully autonomous competitive games whose entire lifecycle - open, run, resolve, reward, burn, reset - executes on server-side automation with zero human intervention required.

  • Showdown (Automated Prediction Market): Community members wager on daily head-to-head matchups. Winners receive losers' stakes; a platform fee is permanently removed from supply. The full cycle runs entirely on automation. No admin required.
  • Real // Rebased (Daily Prediction Game): Participants spend community currency to predict hidden daily outcomes. Correct predictions receive multiplied rewards. Asset holders receive a built-in bonus. Full 24-hour cycle executes without any human trigger.
  • Partner Revenue Pipeline: External communities pay to run branded events on the platform. They supply assets and prize pool; the platform handles all operations. Zero custom development per client - pure recurring revenue.
Business Impact

Removes operational dependency entirely - the platform runs correctly for weeks without admin access. The partner pipeline converts a single-community tool into a monetisable infrastructure business with recurring B2B revenue.

BTFD
Rabbits

Holder Retention Platform · Closed Currency Economy · Anti-Churn Mechanics · Ethereum

The Problem

An existing Ethereum asset community experiencing holder attrition - assets listed for sale with increasing frequency, no utility layer rewarding long-term participation.

The Solution

A complete utility infrastructure layer with an asset commitment mechanic, a closed-loop community currency, and real-time listing detection that converts the first step toward selling into an ongoing financial cost.

  • Asset Listing Penalty (Anti-Sell Mechanic): The system monitors the secondary market in real time. Any asset actively listed for sale stops earning community currency immediately. Listing now has a direct, continuous financial cost. Floor sell pressure drops without community policing.
  • Closed-Loop Community Currency: The currency exists only inside the platform - it cannot be withdrawn or traded externally. Three internal spending destinations create ongoing engagement reasons while preventing value from leaking out of the ecosystem.
  • Configurable Prize Machine: A randomised reward mechanic where holders spend currency for prize draws. Odds, prizes, and reward tiers are adjustable from admin panel with no code changes - enabling seasonal promotions without developer involvement.
Business Impact

Directly addresses holder attrition by making the listing decision financially costly. The closed currency loop ensures all spending activity drives engagement within the ecosystem.

PunkLocker
+ Punk Power

Long-Term Loyalty System · Compounding Commitment Mechanics · Base Blockchain

The Problem

No structural way to distinguish long-term committed community members from short-term speculators, or to create a compounding financial cost to exiting - without forcing holders or relying on their goodwill.

The Solution

A voluntary asset commitment vault where holding time automatically converts into a loyalty score (Punk Power) that accrues passively and harvests into spendable platform currency. Leaving early forfeits accumulated points.

  • Time-Based Loyalty Accumulation: Punk Power accrues per committed asset per day - automatically, no action required. The longer the commitment, the more valuable the position becomes. Converts patience into a compounding financial advantage.
  • Secure Blockchain-to-Platform Bridge: Accumulated on-chain loyalty score converts to spendable platform currency through a verification process requiring multiple blockchain confirmations before crediting - preventing exploitation through chain reorganisation.
  • Non-Transferable Utility Balance: The earned balance cannot be sent to other wallets, traded, or withdrawn. Earned exclusively by holding and spendable only within the platform - ensuring utility remains exclusive to genuine long-term participants.
Business Impact

Creates structural loyalty without coercion. The compounding loyalty curve converts speculative asset holders into long-term participants with an increasing financial stake in staying - and an increasing cost to leaving.

$JUNK
Economy

Deflationary Community Currency · Fixed Supply · Dual-Layer Architecture · Base

The Problem

Community currencies issued without supply controls inflate predictably. Tokens accumulate without circulating, holders build exit positions quietly, then liquidate simultaneously - destroying the currency's utility in a coordinated event.

The Solution

A fixed-supply community currency (1 billion tokens, no new issuance ever possible) with a deflationary mechanism where every platform interaction permanently removes a percentage from total supply. More activity equals less supply.

  • Fixed Supply with Five Distribution Channels: 1 billion tokens maximum - no further minting is architecturally possible. Distribution routes through five independently managed channels, each toggleable and publicly verifiable on-chain.
  • Activity-Driven Supply Reduction: Every game fee and platform transaction permanently destroys a small percentage of spent tokens. More platform engagement equals less circulating supply. Scarcity is a function of participation.
  • Two-Layer Architecture (Blockchain + Internal Ledger): The blockchain layer handles distribution and public visibility. The internal layer handles all gameplay spending - preventing exploitation while preserving transparency.
Business Impact

Creates a self-reinforcing economy where community participation directly benefits all holders by reducing supply. The fixed cap and burn mechanism eliminate the inflation risk that has destroyed most Web3 game economies.

The Secret
World

Persistent Idle World · Three-Tier Economy · Community Governance · Ethereum

The Problem

A small digital asset community (555 assets) needed a persistent game environment that remained active between sessions, gave holders ongoing reasons to return, and reduced operational dependence on the founding team.

The Solution

A continuously-running browser world featuring a three-tier currency progression system, self-expiring yield assets that create natural daily return cycles, and a community governance layer that distributes operational decisions to participants.

  • Three-Tier Progression Currency: The economy operates across three currency tiers in a 100:1 conversion ratio. Each tier unlocks progressively different activities, extending meaningful engagement arc across weeks and preventing early ceiling-hitting.
  • Self-Expiring Yield Assets (Idle Loop): Free temporary items planted in the game world generate rewards as they grow, then expire permanently. Players return to harvest and replant - a natural idle loop generating daily return behaviour without requiring new content.
  • Player-Governed World Mechanics: A community congress where participants vote on active rewards, event schedules, and currency rates. Operational decisions become community engagement events rather than founder overhead.
Business Impact

Enables a small team to run a continuously-engaging game world with minimal operational overhead. The governance layer converts administrative decisions into community engagement events, reducing founder burnout while increasing participant investment.

Vault

Community Intelligence Platform · Holder Behaviour Analytics · Multi-Chain

The Problem

Ecosystem operators lack tools to understand who their community actually is. Standard blockchain analytics show wallet addresses and transaction counts - not behavioural patterns, loyalty signals, or churn risk indicators.

The Solution

A wallet-connected analytics platform that profiles community members by behaviour rather than just address - segmenting holders into actionable tiers and surfacing churn signals before they show up in the floor price.

  • Behavioural Segmentation Engine: Automatically categorises every wallet based on portfolio composition, transaction history, and activity patterns - distinguishing committed long-term holders from recent buyers, flagging inactive wallets at churn risk.
  • Historical Portfolio Intelligence: View any holder's portfolio composition at any point in the past. Enables retroactive reward eligibility, loyalty milestone verification, and understanding how community composition changed around key events.
  • Multi-Chain Holder Profiles: Activity and portfolio history indexed continuously across Base and Ethereum. Cross-chain holder profiles from a single dashboard - no manual reconciliation required.
Business Impact

Converts raw blockchain data into actionable community intelligence. Enables operators to run precision re-engagement campaigns, design retroactive loyalty rewards, and identify churn signals before the community publicly notices them.

Modular NFT
Gaming System

Extensible Game Contract Framework · Add Features Without Redeployment

The Problem

Web3 gaming studios must redeploy entire smart contracts every time they add a feature. Each redeployment is expensive, disruptive to existing holders, and creates compounding technical debt as the product evolves.

The Solution

A modular smart contract framework that separates each game feature into an independent, upgradeable module. Adding or replacing any feature doesn't require touching the core asset contract - eliminating redeployment costs entirely.

  • Swappable Feature Modules: Game features - player profiles, inventory, progression points, in-game currency, asset rentals - are built as independent modules connected through a central controller. New features plug in; old features upgrade without redeployment.
  • Verifiable On-Chain Progression: Each digital asset tracks its own progression points directly on the blockchain. When a player completes an action, the system updates the asset's on-chain record. Advancement is publicly verifiable - any holder can prove their asset's history.
  • Multi-Chain Revenue Infrastructure: Built-in creator royalty enforcement at the contract level - every secondary sale automatically pays the configured creator percentage. Deployable across 10+ blockchain networks from a single codebase.
Business Impact

Eliminates the compounding technical debt and cost of redeployment cycles. Studios iterate on game mechanics without the disruption and expense of redeploying core asset contracts - dramatically reducing the cost of product evolution.

Decentralised
NFT Marketplace

Trustless Trading Infrastructure · Contract-Enforced Creator Royalties · 10 Blockchains

The Problem

Centralised digital asset marketplaces have repeatedly eliminated creator royalties on secondary sales when competitive pressure increases - depriving creators of a critical revenue stream they built their economics around.

The Solution

A trustless trading platform where the transaction logic - including creator royalty distribution - is enforced by the smart contract itself. No marketplace operator can override, reduce, or eliminate the royalty payout.

  • Non-Custodial Transaction Escrow: When a seller lists an asset, it transfers to a neutral smart contract - neither party controls it. Buyer completes the purchase → contract simultaneously delivers the asset and payment in one atomic transaction. If anything fails, both parties automatically recover their assets.
  • Contract-Level Royalty Enforcement: Creator royalty percentages are written into the contract at deployment. Every secondary sale automatically routes the specified percentage to the creator - executes at the protocol layer. No platform can opt out once the contract is deployed.
  • Cross-Chain Revenue Infrastructure: The same architecture deploys across 10 major blockchain networks with live pricing from price oracles for accurate current-value display. One codebase, 10 deployment targets.
Business Impact

Returns royalty revenue control to creators at the infrastructure level. For projects with significant secondary market volume, contract-enforced royalties represent the difference between a sustainable creator economy and a depleting one.
